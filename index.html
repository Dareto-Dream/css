Usage

In your <head>
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://css.deltavdevs.com/fonts.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://css.deltavdevs.com/theme.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://css.deltavdevs.com/themes.css" />

Set data-theme="teal" on <html>. The 3D demo uses the same theme tokens to paint its world.
